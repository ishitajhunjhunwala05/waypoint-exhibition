Project Statement

College of Isolation is a fictional archive that asks: what if the university were honest about what it actually is? Not a community, not a creative haven — but a space of isolation, observation, and managed experience.

Drawing on the aesthetics of institutional documentation, this project constructs a parallel version of UAL that says out loud what is usually left unsaid.
